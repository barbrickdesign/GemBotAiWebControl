## The "Trouble Spot" Feature (New!)

If you ask about the same topic 3+ times:

**Visit 1**: "How do I control speed?"  
**Visit 5**: "What about speed levels?"  
**Visit 8**: "Speed control again..."  

→ **Merlin identifies**: You're struggling with speed control

→ **Next greeting**: "I notice speed control has been on your mind.  
                      Would you like me to explain differently?"

**This is the big change.** Merlin now offers *targeted help*, not generic advice.
