R2D2 Kit Card - with moving parts by dymitboss on Thingiverse: https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:7062192

Summary:
Who has never wanted to build their own R2D2??Now you can do it!!!To print the kit:R2D2_kitCard_Part1R2D2_kitCard_Part2(the rest are individual pieces in case you need them for some reason)Due to the structure and parts that allow movement, it is not possible for the design to be completely flat, but it still comes in a kit card format and is great for a fun time assembling this model or even to give as a gift.All parts are fitted with the mounting pins or already have their own fitting on the part (like the connection between legs and feet).The "cameras" on the head are mounted with some pressure. Assembly is quite simple.Due to the level of detail the model is a little big, but it can be printed smaller at the cost of some detail.I spent about 3 weeks and many hours doing this project, I believe that in the end it was worth it because I managed to make it articulated a model which was really my goal. I had to redesign the pins at least 4 times, and before that ad to try out a few different ideas.
