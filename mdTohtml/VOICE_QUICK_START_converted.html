GemBot Voice Feature - Quick Start

🔊 Voice is Now Active!

Turn Voice On/Off

Click 🔊 Voice ON button in the voice panel (below AI chat area)

  • Shows 🔊 Voice ON (green) = Voice enabled
  • Shows 🔇 Voice OFF (red) = Voice disabled

Choose Your Voice

  1. Open 🎤 Voice dropdown
  2. Select voice (e.g., "Google US English Female")
  3. Next AI response uses that voice

Adjust Speed

Drag ⚡ Speed slider left-right

  • 0.5x = Very slow (clear, careful)
  • 1.0x = Normal (default)
  • 2.0x = Double speed (fast feedback)

Adjust Pitch

Drag 🎵 Pitch slider left-right

  • 0.5 = Deep voice
  • 1.0 = Normal (default)
  • 2.0 = High-pitched

Adjust Volume

Drag 🔈 Volume slider left-right

  • 0% = Silent (text only)
  • 50% = Quiet background
  • 100% = Full volume

Watch Status

🔊 Speaking... = AI is talking (orange glow)

🔊 Ready = Listening for next question

What Gets Spoken

✅ AI responses (all assistant messages)

❌ Your questions (text only)

❌ System messages (connection, mode change)

❌ Error messages

Examples

You: "Where am I?"

You: "What's my speed?"


AI (spoken): "Speed Level 3 slash 5 20 milliseconds Standard cutting speed"



You: "Help me move"

Tips

  1. Hands-free: Enable voice, ask questions, hands stay on controls
  2. Faster feedback: Increase speed (1.5-2x) for quick responses
  3. Deep voice: Lower pitch (0.5-0.8) for distinct personality
  4. Quiet operation: Lower volume (30-50%) for background feedback
  5. Custom combo: Try 1.5x speed + 1.2 pitch for confident tone
